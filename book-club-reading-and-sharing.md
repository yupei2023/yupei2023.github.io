## Thematic sharing series not counted as books

The archive also contains substantial multiweek sharing series that were not presented as readings of one identifiable published book. They should be documented separately as public scholarship or community learning activities rather than placed in the book table:

- *Human–Computer Interaction Research in Educational Context* by Yupei Duan, November 2024 — a series of reflections on learning and research experiences in the Information Science and Learning Technologies Ph.D. program
- *AI and Education* / 《AI与教育》, curated and presented by Yupei Duan, March 2025 — a learning-reflection series based on recently published research about artificial intelligence and education
- “学习动机从哪来？”, May 2025
- 《那些反复听的话》, August 2025 (a curated set of speeches, essays, and talks)

The folder `学习社群分享（备用）《Beyond Feelings》` was also excluded because its “备用” label does not establish that a sharing cycle took place.
