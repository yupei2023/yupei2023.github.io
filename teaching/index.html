Teaching philosophy

Listen, learn, and liberate.

Technology has educational value only through its relationship with the learner, task, teacher, and purpose.

01

Listen

Learners bring knowledge, identities, questions, aspirations, and different ways of interpreting the world. Their questions, work, reflection, and feedback are intellectual resources—not labels.

02

Learn

Every lesson and technological intervention is a design hypothesis. Evidence from student work, discussion, observation, and assessment should guide revision.

03

Liberate

The purpose of support is increasing independence: learners who can question, verify, reason, decide responsibly, and continue learning beyond the classroom.

Listening produces evidence. Evidence guides learning and revision. Thoughtful support helps learners assume greater agency—and their new questions begin the cycle again.

Sunshine does not command growth; it creates conditions in which growth becomes possible.

AI, immersive environments, simulations, and games can make learning accessible, interactive, and meaningful. They should deepen learning, cross genuine barriers, preserve privacy and ethical judgment, and strengthen rather than replace learner agency.
