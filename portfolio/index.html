Integrative reflection

My doctoral development has not been a departure from teaching. It has been a process of learning to investigate, design, and communicate more systematically while keeping learners’ experiences at the center.

Across coursework and research, I have learned to connect educational problems with appropriate evidence; to treat technology as part of a larger learning system; to interpret quantitative, qualitative, behavioral, and interaction data together; and to keep claims proportionate to the evidence.
