Decision Log

Every time AI helps you, one question decides whether you learned anything: what did you keep, what did you drop — and why? Log each consultation here as you work. When you're done, copy the log into your assignment — it is the written evidence of your judgment at work. Log the consequential consultations — the ones where a real decision was made — not every exchange.

Everything you type stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded, ever. Your draft is saved automatically.

What good looks like: the quality of your reasons matters more than which decision you made. A well-explained “accept” beats a thoughtless “reject.” Cite the criterion or the evidence that convinced you — and keep it light: one or two consequential entries beat ten routine ones. Your instructor may ask for exactly that.
