P-FACET

When AI helps you, who is doing the thinking?当 AI 帮助你时，究竟是谁在思考？

P-FACET is a six-phase routine for using generative AI without giving away the thinking that matters. P-FACET 是一个包含六个阶段的生成式 AI 使用模型，帮助学习者借助 AI，同时保留真正重要的思考过程。

The model模型介绍

Keep cognition visible and learner-owned.让思考过程可见，并由学习者掌控。

Generative AI can frame problems, draft explanations, propose solutions, and revise work. P-FACET helps educators and learners decide which cognitive acts must remain human-led—and how AI can provide bounded, productive support.生成式 AI 可以界定问题、撰写解释、提出方案并修改作品。P-FACET 帮助教育者与学习者判断：哪些认知活动必须由人主导，以及 AI 应如何提供有边界、有效的支持。

P

Problem-Centered Challenge以问题为中心的挑战

Anchor the work in an authentic problem, purpose, and learning goal.以真实问题、明确目的和学习目标作为整个过程的核心。

F

Frame界定

Define goals, constraints, assumptions, stakeholders, and criteria.明确目标、限制条件、假设、利益相关者与评价标准。

A

Analogize & Attempt类比与尝试

Connect prior knowledge and make a human-first attempt.联系已有知识，并先独立完成初步尝试。

C

Consult & Construct咨询与建构

Ask AI for bounded help: critique, alternatives, questions, or hints.向 AI 寻求有边界的帮助，如批评、替代方案、问题或提示。

E

Evaluate & Explain评估与解释

Verify outputs, apply evidence, and justify what you accept or reject.核查 AI 输出、运用证据，并说明接受、修改或拒绝的理由。

T

Transform & Transfer转化与迁移

Revise the work and carry the strategy into a new context.转化和完善成果，并将形成的策略迁移到新的情境。

P remains the anchor. F-A-C-E-T is an iterative cycle, not a mechanical checklist. Only C requires direct AI consultation.P 始终是核心锚点。F-A-C-E-T 是一个可反复迭代的循环，而不是机械清单。只有 C 阶段需要直接与 AI 互动。

Two perspectives双重视角

A shared language for teaching and learning with AI.为 AI 时代的教与学建立共同语言。

For educators面向教育者

Design for evidence of thinking.围绕思考证据进行教学设计。

Identify the cognitive work an assignment is meant to develop. Use P-FACET to set appropriate boundaries for AI, prompt explanation and verification, and assess process alongside product.识别一项学习任务真正要培养的认知能力。运用 P-FACET 设定适当的 AI 使用边界，引导学习者解释与核查，并同时评价过程与结果。

  • Clarify what must remain learner-owned明确哪些思考必须由学习者完成
  • Make framing, judgment, and revision visible让问题界定、判断与修改过程可见
  • Distinguish support from substitution区分认知支持与认知替代

For learners面向学习者

Use AI without surrendering agency.借助 AI，同时保持学习自主性。

Begin with your own representation and attempt. Consult AI for a specific need, evaluate its response with evidence, transform the work, and explain what you can use again.从自己的理解与尝试出发，针对具体需要向 AI 咨询；运用证据评估其回答，转化成果，并说明哪些方法能够再次使用。

  • Make your own representation first — a draft, sketch, or prediction先形成自己的表征——初稿、草图或预测
  • Request critique, options, or hints—not the whole task寻求批评、选项或提示，而非让 AI 完成整个任务
  • Accept, revise, or reject with reasons有理由地接受、修改或拒绝 AI 输出
