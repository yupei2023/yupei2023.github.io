Bounded Request Builder

The difference between AI that replaces your thinking and AI that sharpens it is mostly written in the request. Answer three questions about your own work first — this page assembles the bounded prompt.

Everything stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded. Your draft saves automatically.

1

What have you already attempted?

Your draft, plan, sketch, or prediction — in a sentence, or pasted in full. In an Extended task, your criteria for judging the AI's draft count too. This is your A phase.

Not yet? Then AI isn't the next step — the attempt is. Even a sketch, a prediction, or a list of what you know counts. Try first; this page will be here.
2

What exactly do you need?

Name the specific gap — the narrower, the better the help.

3

What role should AI play?

One job. In Standard tasks, AI consults — it never authors. (An Extended task may deliberately widen this; follow your assignment's designation.)
