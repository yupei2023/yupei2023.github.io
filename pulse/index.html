How selection works

Recent. Relevant. Credible.

  1. 01
    Collect

  2. 02
    Evaluate

  3. 03
    Balance

    Deduplicate records and diversify the final ten across themes and publication venues.

Editorial note

A discovery aid, not an endorsement.

Pulse is designed to use bibliographic metadata for automated discovery. Inclusion will not imply endorsement, and access labels will distinguish open full text from publisher pages that may require a subscription.
